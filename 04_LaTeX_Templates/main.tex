			\item \textbf{Equation:}
			\begin{equation}
				\ln\left(\frac{p}{1-p}\right) = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X_{EF} + \beta_2 X_{Cr} + \epsilon

			\item \textbf{Significance:} Model achieved an ROC-AUC of 0.862.
